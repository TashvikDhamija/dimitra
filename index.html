AI killed the video star

Authors: Baptiste Chopin, Tashvik Dhamija, Yaohui Wang, Antitza Dantcheva

Abstract

We propose Dimitra++, a novel framework for audio-driven talking head generation, streamlined to learn lip motion, facial expression, as well as head pose motion. Specifically, we train a conditional Motion Diffusion Transformer (cMDT) by modeling facial motion sequences with 3D representation. We condition the cMDT with only two input signals, an audio-sequence, as well as a reference facial image. Quantitative and qualitative experiments on two widely employed datasets, VoxCeleb2 and CelebV-HQ, showcase that Dimitra++ is able to outperform existing approaches for generating realistic talking heads imparting lip motion, facial expression, and head pose.
